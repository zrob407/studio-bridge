What it does

Not a dashboard. A toolbox.

These are the jobs that take the most clicking by hand and the least time with Studio Bridge.

01 · BULK EDITING

Edit hundreds of things in one step

batch runs up to 50 edits as a single undo step. instance_bulk sets, adjusts or deletes everything that matches a filter, with dry runs first.

  • Rename, duplicate in rows, grids and circles
  • Snap to a grid and change pivots
batch1 call
instance_rename40 parts
instance_duplicatea circle of copies
snap_gridalign to the grid
instance_bulkdry run, then apply
one undo step
02 · WORLD BUILDING

Build a world quickly

terrain generates hills with a biome, water level and seed. environment switches lighting between day, sunset, night and fog. scatter drops props with spacing and slope alignment.

  • spatial_find finds flat spots, free space and spawn points
  • spatial_check tells you what floats or overlaps
worldone prompt
terrainhillswater levelsunsetnightfogscatterspatial_find
Lighting presets
daysunsetnightfog
03 · UI

UI that fits every device

ui_build makes a whole GUI from one description. ui_autoscale turns a pixel-based GUI into responsive Scale. ui_responsive_check lays it out on real phones, tablets and consoles.

  • Reports what runs off the screen
  • What is too small to tap, and text that is too tiny
ui_responsive_checkdevice emulator
iPhoneiPadXbox
It checks for
off screenparts that run out of view
tap sizebuttons too small to press
texttiny or unreadable text
04 · PLAYTESTS

Playtest without touching the keyboard

character_move walks the character to a spot with pathfinding. input_keyboard and input_mouse send real input, gui_click presses buttons by path, and run_test reports what happened.

playtesthands off
character_movewalk to the shop door
gui_clickpress Buy
run_testreport the errors
no keyboard needed

Local by design

Easy to trust

The bridge runs on your computer and talks only to your own Studio.

127.0.0.1 only

It listens only on your own machine and needs a secret on every request.

Signed plugin

The plugin's signature is verified, and releases ship with build provenance.

No telemetry

Nothing about you or your places is collected.

No forced restarts

Every command has a deadline and can be cancelled, and results survive a reconnect.
