How it works

  1. Buy a license

    Checkout is handled by Polar, the seller of record. You get a license key by email.

  2. Activate it

    Open the Pro tab in the Studio Bridge window, paste the key and press Activate. Or run studio-bridge pro activate, which asks for the key with the typing hidden. Pro needs Studio Bridge 0.6.7 or newer.

  3. Use the tools

    The packs download automatically and their tools appear in your AI tool. studio-bridge pro status shows what is installed, and studio-bridge pro remove takes the license off this computer and frees its device slot.

What is sent, and what is not

Sent to the license server

Your license key, a random device id the program creates, your computer's name (shown as the device label on your Polar purchases page) and your internet address.

Never sent

Your places, scripts, file names or anything else you work on.

Works offline

After activating, packs keep working offline for up to three days. They check in again every so often.

A separate license

Pro has its own license, apart from the free program's. It covers devices, what you may and may not share, plan length, privacy and refunds. Read it in plain terms on the license page, or the full Pro license text.
