9. Privacy and the license server
The packs contact the Studio Bridge license server to check your key and to download
the pack code. The server receives your license key, a random device id the packs create
on your computer, your computer's name (shown as the device label on your Polar
purchases page) and your internet address. It stores only a one-way hash of your key and
of the device id, the Polar activation id, a short-lived record of which networks a key
was used from to catch key sharing, and a count of failed attempts per hashed network and
device to stop key guessing. Failed-attempt counts expire after 7 days and lockout
records after at most 30 days. Your key is also checked with Polar, which sells
the licenses. The packs never send your Roblox places, scripts, file names or anything
else you work on to the server.
